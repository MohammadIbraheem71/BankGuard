# Fraud and Security Alerts

## How the bank contacts you
The bank sends security alerts by SMS, email or an in-app notification. A genuine alert never asks for your password, PIN or one-time code, and never asks you to move money to a safe account.

## Suspicious messages
Do not open links in messages that claim your account will be closed. Type the bank address directly into your browser or use the official app instead.

## Reporting fraud
Call the 24-hour helpline immediately if you see a transaction you did not make. Block your card in the app first if the transaction was card-based. A written complaint can be filed at any branch.

## One-time codes
A one-time code is only for signing in or confirming a payment you started. Sharing a code with a caller allows them to take over your session.

## Account monitoring
Enable debit alerts for all amounts. Review your monthly statement even if you receive alerts. The bank is not liable for losses reported more than ninety days after the transaction.
