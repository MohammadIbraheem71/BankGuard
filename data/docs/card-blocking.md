# Card Blocking and Replacement

## Block a card immediately
If a card is lost, stolen or used without permission, block it from the mobile app by opening Cards, selecting the card and tapping Block Card. The block takes effect immediately and cannot be reversed by the customer.

## Blocking by phone
Call the 24-hour helpline and choose the card services menu. An agent will verify your CNIC and the last four digits of the card number before blocking it.

## Temporary freeze
A temporary freeze can be lifted by the customer. Use it when you misplace a card temporarily. A permanent block requires a replacement card.

## Replacement cards
Replacement cards are dispatched within three working days to the address on file. Standard replacement costs PKR 500. A replacement after a reported fraud is free.

## After blocking
Recurring card payments fail once the card is blocked. Update any merchant subscriptions with the new card number after activation. Any cash withdrawal made before the block remains your responsibility.
