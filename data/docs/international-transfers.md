# International Transfers

## Sending money abroad
International transfers can be made through the mobile app or at a branch. You need the recipient's full name, bank name, SWIFT or IBAN code and country. Transfers are processed on working days only.

## Fees and exchange rates
The sending fee is PKR 2,500 for transfers up to the equivalent of USD 1,000 and PKR 4,000 above that. The exchange rate shown at the time of confirmation is held for ten minutes.

## Limits
The annual limit for a standard customer is the equivalent of USD 25,000. Business customers can apply for a higher limit with supporting documents.

## Tracking a transfer
A transfer reference number is issued immediately. Use it in the app to see whether the payment is processing, completed or returned. Completed transfers usually arrive within two working days.

## Returns
If the recipient's bank rejects the payment, the amount is credited back to the source account within three working days. The sending fee is not refunded.
