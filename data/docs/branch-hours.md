# Branch Hours

## Regular hours
BankGuard Demo Bank branches are open Monday to Friday from 09:00 to 17:00 and on Saturday from 09:00 to 13:00. Branches are closed on Sunday and public holidays.

## Cash services
Cash deposits and withdrawals are accepted until 16:30 on weekdays and 12:30 on Saturdays. After those times, customers should use an ATM or the night deposit box.

## Private banking desks
Private banking appointments are available between 10:00 and 16:00 on weekdays. Walk-in customers are seen only if a banker is available.

## Holiday schedule
The bank follows the national public holiday calendar. On the day before a public holiday, branches close one hour early. The helpline remains available on all holidays.

## Finding a branch
Use the branch locator in the mobile app to see opening hours, wheelchair access and whether a branch has a cash vault. The locator works offline once the app has cached your city.
