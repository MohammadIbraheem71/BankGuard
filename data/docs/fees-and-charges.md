# Fees and Charges

This page summarises the standard fee schedule for BankGuard Demo Bank accounts. All figures are illustrative and apply only to this synthetic training environment.

## Monthly account fee
A checking account has no monthly maintenance fee when at least one salary credit posts during the calendar month. Otherwise a flat PKR 250 fee is charged on the first working day of the following month. Savings accounts are fee-free at all times.

## Transaction charges
- ATM withdrawal from a BankGuard machine: free.
- ATM withdrawal from another network: PKR 150 per transaction.
- Over-the-counter cash withdrawal at a branch: free up to PKR 100,000 per day.
- Cheque book: PKR 400 for 25 leaves.
- Duplicate statement request: PKR 200 per statement.

## Transfer charges
Internal transfers between two BankGuard accounts are free. Local bank transfers through the RAFT or IBFT network cost PKR 100 for amounts up to PKR 50,000 and PKR 250 above that. International transfer fees are listed in the international transfers guide.

## Waivers
Students and senior customers can request a fee waiver at any branch. The waiver removes monthly and over-the-counter charges but does not cover third-party network fees.
