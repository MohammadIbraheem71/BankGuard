# Mobile App Guide

## Signing in
The app uses a six-digit PIN or biometric login. After a new device is registered, a one-time code is sent to the registered phone number.

## Main screens
The home screen shows account balances and the five most recent transactions. The Payments screen holds saved beneficiaries and scheduled transfers. The Cards screen controls card freezes and limits.

## Notifications
Push notifications can be enabled for every debit, for debits above a chosen amount, or for card activity only. Notifications are informational and do not replace the statement.

## Offline access
The app can display cached balances and the last twenty transactions while offline. Payments cannot be made offline.

## Troubleshooting
If the app shows an outdated balance, pull down on the home screen to refresh. If the problem persists, sign out and sign in again. Do not install the app from links in messages.
