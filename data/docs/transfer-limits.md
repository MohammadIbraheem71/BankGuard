# Transfer Limits

Transfer limits are applied per customer, per calendar day, and reset at midnight local time.

## Internal transfers
Transfers between two BankGuard accounts are limited to PKR 500,000 per day for a standard checking account and PKR 200,000 per day for a savings account. There is no per-transaction charge.

## External local transfers
RAFT and IBFT transfers are limited to PKR 250,000 per day. Each individual transfer must be at most PKR 100,000 unless the customer has completed in-branch identity verification.

## Cardless transfers
A cardless transfer to a phone number is capped at PKR 25,000 per transaction and PKR 75,000 per day. The recipient must collect the amount within 72 hours.

## Raising a limit
Customers can request a temporary limit increase at a branch with a valid CNIC and a purpose letter. Approved increases are valid for 30 days. Large transfers may be held for a compliance review before they are credited.
