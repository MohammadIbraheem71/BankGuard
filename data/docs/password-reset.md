# Password Reset

## Reset from the mobile app
Open the sign-in screen, tap Forgot Password, and enter your registered email address or phone number. A one-time code is sent to the registered channel. Codes expire after five minutes.

## Choosing a new password
Use at least twelve characters with a mix of letters, numbers and symbols. Do not reuse a password from another site. The bank will never ask for your password by phone, email or chat.

## Locked accounts
After five failed sign-in attempts the account is locked for fifteen minutes. The lock clears automatically. If you are travelling, enable travel notice in the app to avoid extra verification.

## Code not received
Check that your registered phone number or email address is current. If it is not, visit a branch with your CNIC to update the contact details. The helpline cannot reset a password without the one-time code.

## After a reset
A successful password reset signs out all active sessions, including the mobile app and the web banking portal. Reconfirm any scheduled transfers after signing in again.
