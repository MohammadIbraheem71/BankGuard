# Account Types

## Checking account
A checking account is designed for everyday payments. It supports unlimited internal transfers, a debit card, bill payments and ATM access. A minimum balance is not required.

## Savings account
A savings account pays a modest monthly profit rate and is intended for funds that are not used daily. External transfers from a savings account are capped at PKR 200,000 per day.

## Joint account
A joint account can be opened by two customers. Both account holders can view balances and statements. Any transfer above PKR 100,000 requires confirmation from both holders.

## Business account
A business account supports payroll uploads, multi-user access and higher transfer limits. It requires a business registration number and a signed mandate.

## Opening an account
Bring a valid CNIC, a recent photograph and proof of address to any branch. The account is activated after identity verification. The debit card arrives within three working days.
